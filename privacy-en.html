Cuevaro Privacy Policy Updated: 16 November 2026 This policy describes data processing for third-party switcher control, video reception and local production settings. Developer: 李兆哲. Support and privacy contact: ipad@live.it. 1. Local storage The app stores switcher addresses, preferences, panel layouts and macro remarks on your device. Video-source URLs may contain credentials and are stored in the iOS Keychain rather than ordinary preferences. Keychain items may survive uninstalling the app. Media-pool thumbnails are cached during a session. The app processes media you explicitly select through the system photo/file picker and upload controls. 2. Network connections and recipients Connecting to a switcher sends control commands to your chosen device and receives device state. Connecting to a video source requests media from that source or its provider, which can receive your IP address, requested URL and normal connection information. NDI uses local network discovery and reception; iOS may request local network permission. When you explicitly select and connect to a Tencent webrtc:// source, the app sends the complete stream URL, including signed parameters, and SDP session information to Tencent's WebRTC signaling service to establish reception. Standard WHEP sends SDP to the endpoint you enter. No default public STUN/TURN service is configured. Use authorized, trusted sources. Recipients' logging and retention are governed by their own policies. 3. Purchases and trial The 10-day trial and lifetime unlock use Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles payments and Apple Account information. The app reads verified products, transaction dates, revocations and entitlements to calculate access and restore purchases. The app does not receive card details. The trial does not automatically charge you; lifetime unlock requires your explicit confirmation. 4. Developer services This version does not integrate advertising, developer analytics accounts or a developer-operated media-upload service. Your sources/devices, Apple services and bundled SDK network activity may still involve third-party processing; this does not mean all network participants process no data. NDI and WebRTC license notices are available in the app's license page. 5. Permissions and controls System photo/file pickers provide only your selected items. This version creates no local camera or microphone capture tracks. You can stop preview, delete saved switchers, change source addresses and manage local network permission in iOS Settings. Deleting preferences is different from revoking an Apple transaction; reinstalling does not restart an already claimed trial. 6. Access, corrections and contact You can inspect and change local settings in the app. Contact ipad@live.it for support and privacy requests. Do not send playback keys, passwords or complete signed source URLs. The developer may process contact details and content you voluntarily provide when requesting support. Project backups and imported desktop XML/media are processed locally; the app retains imported project sections on this device for subsequent export. Exported projects may contain production configuration and should only be shared with trusted recipients.
